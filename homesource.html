How much does it cost to hire a Web Developer?

The cost of hiring a WordPress developer will vary depending on:
1) Developer’s Experience: More experienced developers typically command higher rates.

2) Developer’s Location: Geographic location can impact pricing.

3) Project Complexity: More complex projects generally cost more.

Hourly Rates and Project-Based Fees

  • Hourly Rates: Freelance WordPress developers and agencies might charge anywhere between $15 to $250 AUD per hour.
  • Fixed Fees: Both agencies and freelancers may offer project-based pricing. These can range from a few hundred dollars to significant five-figure sums, again showing a wide spectrum.

Stone Digital’s Approach

At Stone Digital, we position ourselves in the ‘sweet middle’ of this spectrum. We offer high-quality services at reasonable rates, avoiding both the excessive prices of over-the-top agencies and the low-quality pitfalls of budget outsourcers. We believe this balance offers the best value for many businesses.

Our custom WordPress web development packages typically start at $8,000 AUD and our monthly Support and Maintenance Plans start from $480 AUD per month.

Transparent Pricing and Consultation

  • Package Pricing: We provide clear, package-based pricing for our services.
  • Initial Consultation: Schedule a consultation with us to find the right package for your project.

How long does it take to build a website?

We generally estimate that a new WordPress project will take between 1.5 to 4 months to complete. This timeline is influenced by several key factors:

  1. Website Complexity: More complex websites require more time to develop.
  2. Information Delivery: The speed at which we receive essential materials like content and images.
  3. Feedback Process: The efficiency of our interactions during the feedback stage.
